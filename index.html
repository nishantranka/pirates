How to Play

⛵ Goal & Controls

You are always the PINK ship, marked with a red ▼ — and every battle opens with a 3-second pause so you can find yourself. Your ship always sails forward — you only steer: ← → or A D. Fire with Space; you carry a magazine, and it reloads itself the moment it runs dry — press R if you'd rather top up early. Submarines dive with ↓ or S (hold). M mutes sound. On touch screens, the whole screen is your steering wheel: hold a finger and your ship sails in that direction (up on the screen = north, right = east…), then lift to keep sailing on that heading. Tap to fire; submarines double-tap to dive or surface. A red arc warns you of incoming shots. Sail off one edge of the map and you emerge seamlessly from the opposite side.

🌬️ Wind

The compass (top-left) shows the wind. Your speed depends on your angle to it: sailing across the wind is fastest (~100%+), straight downwind a bit slower, and beating into the wind crawls at 40%. The wind drifts during battle and affects everyone equally — except submarines, which run on engines and ignore it.

💥 Combat

Broadsides fire from your starboard (right) side only — straight out of the gun muzzles drawn on that rail — so maneuver to bring your right side to bear. You carry a magazine of 3 broadsides (more shells on bigger hulls). Fire it dry and it reloads itself over 2s — or press R at any time to top up early, which costs the same 2s however much was left. Rapid Fire ignores the magazine; 2× Fire doubles it and fires both sides. Damage falls off with distance: point-blank hits hurt ~2.5× more than max-range grazes.

🏝️ Islands

Blocky islands are deadly: the translucent blue ring is shallow water (safe), but touching the sand sinks you instantly. Islands also block cannonballs, so use them as cover — bait pursuers through tight gaps.

🚤 Ramming

Drive your bow into an enemy's side or stern to spike them: they take 3 hull damage and you take 1 in return. Meet bow-to-bow (the whole curved front counts as the bow) and both ships take 3. Glancing side-on scrapes deal nothing — they just shove the hulls apart.

🎁 Power-ups

Labeled bounties bob in the water, often just off a lethal shore (risk vs reward). Surface ships and surfaced subs can grab them:

  • ➕ +1 Health — heals one point (spawns often)
  • ⛨ Shield ×4 — soaks the next 4 shots, but only for 7s — go and use it. An enemy ram shatters it outright: the blow does nothing, and the shield is gone with it
  • ⚡ 2× Speed — double speed for 8s
  • ⇄ 2× Fire — yours to keep for 10s: every volley fires from both sides at once (a doubled magazine), with gold muzzles showing on both rails while it lasts (subs: torpedo spread)
  • ⁘ Rapid Fire — your next shot opens 5s of continuous fire that ignores your magazine

🤿 Submarine

Available in practice and multiplayer: engine-powered (ignores wind), fires a single bow torpedo that runs across the entire map and hits for 2× a cannonball with no distance falloff. Hold ↓/S to dive: while under you're invisible and immune to shots and rams — and you can still fire torpedoes, surfaced or submerged — but you can't grab pickups, and your dive charge (cyan bar) drains — it refills on the surface. Islands still sink you, and the maelstrom still pulls you.

🌀 Maelstrom

A whirlpool forms at the arena's center: an inward current tows every ship toward the middle (stronger outside the shrinking calm eye), so the fight always converges. There's no damage — but you can't out-sail it at full strength. In Leaderboard it holds off until the final 20 seconds, then ramps to full for a sudden-death finish; in Survivor it builds gradually from a short calm.

🏆 Scoring & Battle Modes

You pick the win condition up front in Bots Arena; with friends, the host picks it in the lobby. Leaderboard: a 90-second deathmatch — when you sink you respawn in the middle of the map after a short pause (with a fresh spawn shield), so sink as many as you can before the clock runs out. Bounties are plentiful and spawn fast. For the final 20 seconds the maelstrom sets in: sunk bots stay down for good while human captains keep respawning — a sudden-death scramble. Score = sinking shots (8 pts, weighted most) + damage dealt (1 pt per hull point) + time survived (0.1 pt/s); highest score when time's up wins. Survivor: kills don't matter — only time afloat counts, there are no respawns, and the last one standing wins (bots play cautiously and evade instead of hunting); it ends when one ship remains or every human is dead. Click the 🏆 pill (top-right) for live standings.

🤖 Multiplayer & Bots

Bots Arena on the opening screen drops you straight into a free-for-all against 5, 10 or 15 bots — no room, no waiting. For real crew, pick Play with Friends, create a room and share the 5-letter code — or tap 🔗 Copy invite link and friends just click it to join. No code on hand? The Join a Room screen lists whatever rooms are open right now — tap one to fill in its code. Friends can even join mid-battle — they drop in like a respawn, spawn shield and all. Human captains sail bright colored hulls (yellow, blue, orange, …) while bots are grey, so the real players stand out in a crowd — and you still see your own ship as pink. Or hit Fill with Bots for an instant 10-captain brawl — then +10 Bots if you want a bigger fleet (up to 21 captains). Everyone spawns scattered under a 2s golden spawn shield. If you sink, the round ends — you never spectate bots.

🔴🔵 Team Mode

Toggle it in the lobby (Play with Friends only) for two random Red vs Blue crews instead of a free-for-all — rebalanced fresh every round, Blue mustering a third of the way in from the left, Red a third of the way in from the right, facing off across the water. Hull color now shows your team, so your own ship gets a golden ring instead of the usual pink repaint. A Friendly Fire toggle sits right below it (off by default) — off, shots and rams pass harmlessly through teammates; on, mind your broadsides. Bots on your side never target, rake, ram, or flee from you.
